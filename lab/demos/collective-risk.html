  • N与X_i独立

理论性质：

  • 均值：E[S] = E[N] * E[X]
  • 方差：Var[S] = E[N] * Var[X] + Var[N] * (E[X])²
  • PGF: GS(z) = GN(GX(z))
  • MGF: MS(t) = GN(MX(t))
